# 💡 Idea Document

## Persona
Ravi is a Swiggy delivery partner in Hyderabad earning ₹800 per day.

## Problem Scenario
Due to heavy rainfall or extreme heat, Ravi is unable to work for 1–2 days, resulting in income loss.

## Solution
Valor Shield provides weekly insurance coverage that compensates Ravi automatically when disruptions occur.

## Core Concept
- Weekly subscription model
- Income loss protection
- Automated payout system
